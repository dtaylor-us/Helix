# Glossary

- **Transformation:** A meaningful area of personal change explored over time, not a task to complete perfectly. It holds the experiments, reflections, evidence, and learning connected to that direction.
- **Experiment:** A small, time-bounded behavior tried in order to learn. It names an action, a learning question, and useful evidence; an unexpected result is still useful.
- **Reflection:** A user account of what happened while trying an experiment, including concrete observations, internal responses, and surprises—not merely a success/failure judgment.
- **Evidence:** A specific, provenance-linked observation that supports or challenges a belief. Evidence informs a belief but is not a verdict about the user.
- **Wisdom:** A user-accepted, revisable principle or lesson linked to the reflections or retrospectives that shaped it.
- **Suggested Small Action:** An optional next step generated from current context. It remains a draft the user can accept, dismiss, or replace.
- **Memory:** A durable fact or pattern that may become future context only after user review. AI-derived memories remain proposals and are not diagnoses or permanent facts.
