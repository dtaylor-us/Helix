# Helix Product Vision

Helix helps one person translate vision and identity into practical change through small experiments, reflection, evidence, and wisdom.

Primary outcomes:
- Clarify desired future and life direction.
- Define transformations and goals.
- Run small behavioral experiments.
- Reflect and capture evidence without pressure.
- Build user-owned long-term wisdom.

Helix prioritizes privacy, longitudinal integrity, and calm support over engagement maximization.
