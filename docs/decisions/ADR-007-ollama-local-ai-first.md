# ADR-007 Ollama-compatible local AI first

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-26

## Context
Helix prioritizes privacy and low-cost experimentation.

## Decision
First AI adapter targets local Ollama-compatible endpoint.

## Alternatives
- External provider first.

## Consequences
Reduced external data exposure and cost.

## Risks
Local model quality and latency may vary.

## Reconsideration Triggers
Need for capabilities unavailable in local models with acceptable privacy controls.

## Related Requirements
HELIX-SEC-002, HELIX-AI-001
